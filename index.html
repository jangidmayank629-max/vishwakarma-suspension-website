Founded by the visionary Umesh Kumar, Vishwakarma Shocker Works (VSW) has spent three decades refining the art of suspension engineering. What started as a small workshop in Mahwa has grown into a center of excellence for shock absorber technology.

Our philosophy is simple: precision in every stroke. We believe that a suspension isn't just a part; it's the soul of your vehicle's control. Using state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and 30+ years of hands-on technical data, we ensure every repair meets international performance standards.
